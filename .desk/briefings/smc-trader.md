But by definition those are opposite-direction moves — a sweep wicks beyond one swing extreme and closes back inside; a CHoCH needs the close to break past the *opposite* swing extreme. Requiring both on one 15-minute bar means that single candle needs to wick above the recent high and close below the recent low (or the mirror case) — a shape that essentially never happens. Verified empirically before touching any code: backtested the same-bar check across ~1,600 historical bar-checks on 10 pairs. **Zero hits, ever.** Not "rare" — structurally excluded.

This was never in my own SKILL.md, either — my own framework explicitly describes the sweep and CHoCH as sequential ("CONFIRM CHoCH — on a lower timeframe, immediately after the sweep"), not simultaneous. The mechanical scan's implementation had drifted from the strategy it was supposed to represent.

**Fix**: `dashboard/lib/smc.ts` now has `detectSweepChochConfluence(candles, lookback=5)` — checks for a CHoCH on the current bar, then looks back up to 5 bars for the matching-direction sweep that set it up (sell-side sweep → bullish CHoCH; buy-side sweep → bearish CHoCH), exactly the sequence my own framework describes. Re-tested the same historical data with this window: **31 confluence hits** across the same 1,600 bar-checks — a real, tradeable frequency, still selective (roughly one signal every ~13 hours per pair at this cadence), consistent with "you'd rather miss ten setups than take one that skipped the sweep."

First live run after the fix immediately found a real candidate: SUI/IDR, buy-side sweep + bearish CHoCH within the window. This isn't a loosened strategy — it's the strategy I always had, now actually being checked correctly.
